PROJECT DEEP DIVE

Phishing Triage & Incident Response Playbook

INCIDENT RESPONSE & THREAT ANALYSIS

Engineered a comprehensive incident response playbook and analysis workflow targeting credential compromise via spear-phishing attacks. The framework follows structured guidance from NIST SP 800-61 and maps directly to the MITRE ATT&CK framework (T1566 Phishing, T1078 Valid Accounts).

Incident Overview & Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)

Simulated a scenario where an employee received a phishing email impersonating a trusted service provider, leading to credential harvesting and unauthorized corporate account access. Key IOCs identified include:

  • Suspicious Logins: Login attempts originating from unusual IP addresses or unfamiliar geographic locations.
  • Account Anomalies: Unauthorized email forwarding rules and multi-factor authentication (MFA) fatigue notifications.
  • Outbound Activity: Reports of suspicious or malicious emails transmitted from the compromised employee account.

Benign Baseline Email & SPF Analysis

Analyzed standard corporate email headers (`steampowered.com`) to establish a trusted authentication baseline covering DKIM signatures, SPF pass records, and DMARC enforcement.

Benign Baseline: `Authentication-Results` Header with DKIM, SPF, and DMARC Pass

Dmarcly Verification: Flattened SPF Record and Permitted Sender IP Ranges (`steampowered.com`)

Suspicious Phishing Email & Header Forensics

Inspected raw message headers from a flagged phishing email to isolate mismatching envelope senders (`Return-Path`), source IP addresses, and underlying SPF inclusion structures.

Threat Analysis: `Authentication-Results` Header Showing Source IP `91.232.28.235`

Dmarcly Verification: Flattened SPF Record and IP Block Ingestion (`email.fundscrip.com`)

Structured 5-Stage Incident Response Lifecycle

  • Phase 1 - Acquire & Preserve: Harvested original email headers, authentication logs, VPN records, and identity provider telemetry to establish timeline integrity.
  • Phase 2 - Containment: Executed immediate containment by disabling compromised accounts, forcing credential resets, revoking active session tokens, and purging unauthorized inbox rules.
  • Phase 3 - Eradication: Rotated compromised passwords, enforced strict organization-wide MFA, and updated email security gateway filters to block malicious infrastructure.
  • Phase 4 - Recovery: Verified normal operations, monitored outbound traffic for residual anomalies, and provided targeted user support.
  • Phase 5 - Lessons Learned: Documented root causes (insufficient phishing awareness, weak credential controls) and recommended defensive upgrades such as conditional access policies and recurring simulations.
